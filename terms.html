Disclaimers

Guidance, not advice. Boat & Berth gives general information to help you find, buy and look after a sailboat in the Mediterranean. It is written from experience and public sources, not by a surveyor, lawyer, accountant or the maker of your equipment. Prices, rules, taxes and marina details change; we show the date each section was last checked, but you must confirm anything that matters before you act on it. Scores, fair-value estimates and "likely work" are the app's opinion from listing facts alone, not a condition report. Nothing in the app replaces a professional survey, a sea trial, the engine and equipment manuals, or advice on your own tax and legal position.

Listings. Listings are facts and a link taken from other websites. We do not check them, we are not the seller or the broker, and the original advert is the only authoritative source. Prices may be out of date or converted; VAT status is as stated in the advert unless marked otherwise. A site that wants its listings left out can write to us and they are removed within seven days. Private ads are written by the boat's owner; we do not check them before they go live, and we take no part in the sale.

Directory. Surveyors, insurers, brokers and lawyers are listed with the contact details they publish. A listing is not a recommendation and we receive nothing from them.

Your boat, your responsibility. The maintenance plan and job guides are general. Your boat, engine and equipment may differ, and the maker's manual always takes priority over this app. If a job involves fuel, gas, electricity, the rig, lifting the boat or working aloft or over the side, and you are not sure, stop and use a professional. Intervals are typical, not a warranty.

Training first. These cards are an aide-mémoire for people who have already been trained. They do not replace a radio operator's course, sea-survival or first-aid training, the official procedures of the coast guard where you sail, or the instructions for your own equipment. In an emergency follow the official procedure and the instructions of the authorities. Check the card against current official sources before you rely on it; radio channels and procedures change.

Model track record. The ranking can include each model's general track record for reliability and running costs (better than most, about average or below most), drawn from owner reports, surveys and boat tests. It is a general opinion about the model, not a statement about any particular boat, owner, broker or builder, and it can be switched off in Settings. Always have the boat surveyed.

Estimates and a snapshot. Costs are typical ranges from research in autumn 2026 and your own figures; your actual costs will differ. The admin guide describes rules as we understood them on the date shown; taxes, fees and procedures change in every country, and official sources (the port authority, the tax office, your lawyer or accountant) prevail.
