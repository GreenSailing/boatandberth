Who we are

Boat & Berth Greece ("the app") is published by Christos Leptokaridis, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: boatandberth@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the small amount of personal data described below.

The short version

  • Everything you enter in the app (your buyer profile, shortlist, viewings, boat details, maintenance log, documents, logbook) is stored only on your device. We do not have an account system and we cannot see your data.
  • The app reads public boat listings from the sites you choose, directly from your device, in the same way a web browser would.
  • The free version shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area and the UK, ads are personalised only if you consent; you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • Purchases (the Plus subscription, the Founder option and card packs) are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named here.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location or contacts. The app does not use your location; the marina and harbour map uses fixed positions from our data, not where you are.

Reading listing websites

When you open the Find tab, the app fetches pages from the boat-sale websites you have enabled, from your device, and extracts the facts it shows (model, year, length, price, location, link). It respects each site's robots rules and rate limits and does not get round logins or blocks. Those sites receive an ordinary web request from your device, including your IP address, exactly as if you had visited them in a browser; their own privacy policies apply. The app shows facts and a link only; it does not copy listing photos or full descriptions.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and other device data to show and measure ads. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent the first time it opens and you can withdraw or change it under Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying Plus or the Founder option removes the ads. How Google uses data when you use our app: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play's billing system. Google processes your payment and holds your purchase history under its own privacy policy. We receive only an anonymous confirmation that the purchase is valid for your Google account on this device.

Backups and transfers

If you use Android backup, your app data may be included in your Google account backup under Google's terms. Plus includes an export and backup file that you create and store yourself; we never receive it.

Retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting an item in the app deletes it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to act on them and then deleted.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK you have rights under the GDPR or UK GDPR to access, correct, delete and port your data and to object to or restrict its processing. Since we hold no personal data about you beyond any email you send us, these rights are exercised directly on your device; for emails, write to boatandberth@gmail.com. You may complain to your national data protection authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, www.dpa.gr).

Children

The app is intended for adults buying or owning a boat. It is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). Keep your device updated and locked. The documents vault is only as safe as your phone.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes and show the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the app's "What's new" screen.
