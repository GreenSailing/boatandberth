Find a boat,buy it well,keep it right.

An app for Android and iPhone for buying, then owning, a used sailboat in the Mediterranean.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

Free to use, with in-app purchases for extras.

Find

Boats for sale, ranked for you

  • Answer ten questions about your budget, the size you want and what matters to you.
  • Every boat gets a score against your answers, with the likely work and a fair price.
  • Each boat links to its original ad. The app keeps only the facts: no photos, no ad text.
Buy

Buy it well

  • A viewing checklist that finds the expensive problems before you pay.
  • Questions for the seller, and the papers you should see.
  • First-year costs and the paperwork of each country, step by step.
Keep

Keep it right

  • A maintenance plan that tells you what is due, by date and by engine hours.
  • A log of the work, the costs and the photos, and a service history to print.
  • Marinas and harbours, how-to jobs, and the yearly taxes and fees.

Also in the app

Skipper's course cards

Thirty-three revision cards for the day-skipper syllabus, with drawings and a test mode.

VHF radio trainer

A practice radio with DSC: distress, urgency, safety and routine calls, marked as you speak.

Printable cards

Survey cards, an engine card and a Mayday card with your boat's name and call sign.
