Boat & Berth — Rules for private ads

Version of 10 November 2026.

These rules apply to every private ad placed in Boat & Berth (in the app and on our website). By publishing an ad you accept them.

Who may advertise

  • You must be 18 or over and own the boat, or be authorised by the owner to sell her.
  • The boat must exist, be for sale, and be in the country you choose.
  • Up to three live ads per person. Each ad runs for 90 days and can be renewed.

What an ad must not contain

  • False or misleading facts, prices or photos, or a boat that is not for sale.
  • Photos or text that are not yours to use.
  • Requests for deposits or payments before a viewing, or bank details.
  • Anything illegal, offensive, or unrelated to selling a boat (other goods, services, links to other sales).
  • Other people's personal details.

How we deal with ads

  • We do not check ads before they go live, and we take no part in the sale. Buyers deal with you directly.
  • Anyone can report an ad with "Report this ad". Three reports hide an ad until we have looked at it.
  • We may hide or remove an ad that breaks these rules or the law, or that we reasonably believe is a scam. When we do, we tell you why: the reason is shown with your ad in the app, and we write to the email you signed in with. We may also block repeat offenders from advertising.
  • If you think we got it wrong, reply to that email or write to boatandberth@gmail.com. We look at it again and answer within 14 days.
  • We keep and delete ads and reports as described in the privacy policy.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for your ad and for the sale. Boat & Berth does not guarantee that any ad is accurate or that any sale will happen, and is not liable for dealings between buyers and sellers.

Contact

Questions, complaints and official notices about ads: boatandberth@gmail.com. Notices from authorities in any EU language are accepted; replies are given in English or Greek.
